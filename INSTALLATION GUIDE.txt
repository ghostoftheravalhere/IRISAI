3. FIRST-TIME EYE GAZE CALIBRATION GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------
1. Launch IRIS AI from Desktop shortcut.
2. Click "Calibrate Gaze" on dashboard action bar.
3. Position face in front of camera until face tracking displays Detected.
4. Observe Target 1/9: Top-Left. Look directly at pulsating dot.
5. Click "Sample Point 1/9 ->".
6. Repeat for all 9 points.
7. Observe completion modal: "✓ Calibration Complete".
8. Verify status: "Cursor Control: READY".
9. Click "▶ Start Cursor Control" -> Status changes to "Cursor Control: ACTIVE".
10. Click "⏹ Stop Cursor Control" to pause movement.

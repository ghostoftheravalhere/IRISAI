EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
This guide is written specifically for IBM SkillsBuild Hackathon judges, mentors, evaluators, and end-users who wish to install, evaluate, and demonstrate IRIS AI V2.4 on Windows.

No separate Python or Node.js installation is required.

IRIS AI V2.4 is distributed as a single, standalone, self-contained Windows Installer:

Flow:
Download IRIS-AI-V2.4-Setup.exe -> Run Setup -> Launch IRIS AI -> Ready

3. INSTALLATION STEPS
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1 — Download/Copy:
Obtain IRIS-AI-V2.4-Setup.exe from frontend/release/.

Step 2 — Start Installer:
Double-click IRIS-AI-V2.4-Setup.exe.
Note: If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning ("Windows protected your PC"), click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

Step 3 — Installation Wizard:
Click "Next" on the setup wizard.

Step 4 — Installation Location:
Defaults to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\IRIS AI. Click "Install".

Step 5 — Shortcuts:
The installer automatically creates Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts.

Step 6 — Finish & Launch:
Check "Launch IRIS AI" and click "Finish".

Step 7 — Permissions:
Allow camera and microphone permissions when requested by Windows.

--------------------------------------------------
4. FIRST-TIME EYE GAZE CALIBRATION GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------
1. Launch IRIS AI from the Desktop shortcut.
2. Click "Calibrate Gaze" on the action bar.
3. Position your face in front of camera until face tracking displays Detected.
4. Observe Target 1/9: Top-Left. Look directly at the pulsating dot.
5. Click "Sample Point 1/9 ->".
6. Repeat for all 9 points.
7. Observe completion modal: "✓ Calibration Complete".
8. Verify status displays "Cursor Control: READY".
9. Click "▶ Start Cursor Control" -> Status changes to "Cursor Control: ACTIVE".
10. Click "⏹ Stop Cursor Control" to pause movement.

7. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------
- Camera Not Ready     : Close Zoom/Teams, check Windows Camera privacy, restart IRIS.
- Microphone Not Ready : Check Windows Microphone privacy, click "Retry Microphone".
- Cursor Not Moving    : Complete 9-point calibration, verify READY, click "Start Cursor Control".
- App Command Reports "Not Found" : Requested app is not installed on system. IRIS cleanly reports status.
